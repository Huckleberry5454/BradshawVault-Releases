Bradshaw's Vault

Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

This policy describes how Bradshaw's Vault (the “App”), a personal media-streaming application, handles information when connecting to a privately operated Jellyfin media server.

Information processed

To authenticate users and deliver media, the App processes Jellyfin login information, user identifiers, client or device identifiers, and requests to access media. It exchanges playback information with the configured Jellyfin server, including watched status, resume position, and viewing progress. Search terms may be sent to the server when you search your library. The App may store connection details and authentication tokens on your device to keep you signed in.

How information is used

Information is used for account authentication, streaming, Continue Watching, resume playback, library navigation, and other requested features. The Jellyfin administrator controls the privately hosted server and its user accounts.

Data sharing and external services

Account and playback data is exchanged with the configured privately operated Jellyfin server. We do not sell user information or use it for targeted advertising. The App may contact GitHub to check for software updates or releases; normal technical connection information, such as your IP address, may be processed by GitHub when that happens. Other network infrastructure may process technical connection data to provide connectivity.

Storage and security

Playback information and account settings may remain on the Jellyfin server; authentication and connection information may be retained on your device. Access is controlled through accounts and server settings. No internet-connected service is guaranteed completely secure.

Retention, access, and deletion

Account and playback information may remain on the server while accounts are active or until the server administrator removes it, subject to technical limitations and applicable requirements. To request access, correction, or deletion of associated information, or deletion of an account, email worm2464@gmail.com. Requests will be reviewed and addressed as applicable. Uninstalling the App does not automatically remove server-side records.

Children's privacy

The App is designed for authorized access to a privately operated media server; users cannot independently register public accounts through the App. Parents and guardians can contact the operator about an account or data request.

Changes

If the App's features or data practices change, this policy may be updated. A new effective date will accompany changes.
